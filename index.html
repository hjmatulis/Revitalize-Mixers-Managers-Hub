Protected exceptions: no points

Points apply only to attendance events that are not legally protected and have not been approved as a reasonable accommodation. These never earn points:

  • Leave protected by federal or state law
  • Workers' compensation absences
  • Approved bereavement leave
  • Jury duty
  • Military leave
  • Pre-approved PTO or vacation
  • Approved medical documentation, when required and permitted by law
  • Company-mandated shutdowns or weather closures
  • ADA accommodations
  • PWFA accommodations
